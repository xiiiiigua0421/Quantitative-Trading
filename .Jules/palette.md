## 2025-02-23 - Ensuring keyboard accessibility on scrollable container regions
**Learning:** When using scrollable container regions (`overflow: auto` or `scroll`) without standard interactive elements, users navigating by keyboard often miss or cannot access the content inside them. Screen reader software may also skip over those containers if they lack appropriate attributes.
**Action:** Always ensure keyboard accessibility for scrollable wrapper containers by assigning `tabindex="0"`, identifying them as regions via `role="region"`, and adding a meaningful, localized descriptive label with `aria-label` (e.g., Traditional Chinese for this project). Also ensure there's a visible `:focus-visible` CSS outline applied for visual feedback on focus (using brand color `#16877e`).
